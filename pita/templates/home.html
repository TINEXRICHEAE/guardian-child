On Android, iPhone or a computer

Guardian is an Android app and a web app. On iPhone, iPad, Mac, Windows or Linux, open Guardian in any modern browser; everything works the same.

If your child removes the app, Guardian keeps working: practice messages still arrive by email, and their links open your child's Guardian lessons in the browser, already signed in.
