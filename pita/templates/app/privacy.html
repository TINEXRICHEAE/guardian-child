Privacy

What Guardian keeps

  • Screenshots and files are read once to check them, then thrown away. They are never stored.
  • For a check, Guardian keeps the scam message with secret codes, phone numbers and emails removed.
  • Links are shown as text. Guardian never opens them.
  • Checks run on Guardian's own AI models, not a public chatbot.
  • Reports are saved in Guardian and are not sent to UCC, the Police or 116.
